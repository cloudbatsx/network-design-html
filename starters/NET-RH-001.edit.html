Dual SD-WAN transports into an active/standby security pair and a two-member collapsed core, reconciled against an exact 24U elevation — a comparison artifact at a deliberately smaller scale, with every unverified port, circuit and feed left visibly open.

Section 0

Overview — what this document is

What is different from SJC-HQ?

  • A compact 24U regional-hub rack instead of a 42U headquarters cabinet.
  • Two SD-WAN transports: Internet DIA and private WAN, with LTE as an overlay backup.
  • A smaller campus with two PoE access switches, voice, wireless and IoT endpoints.
  • One local virtualization host for survivable infrastructure services.
  • Product faces and neutral chassis placeholders coexist in the same rack without changing U arithmetic.

Design intent

This is a fictional comparison artifact, not an as-built network. The topology deliberately changes scale, hierarchy and equipment density while keeping the interaction pattern identical: recognizable symbols, restrained hover details, exact rack-unit placement and matched front/rear elevations.

Result to review: whether the template remains readable without relying on the original HQ layout.

Section 3

Rack elevation — SEA.MDF.R01

The physical view uses exact 24U arithmetic and a single device schedule for both faces. Cisco’s FPR-4215 specification was revalidated for this revision and the two firewall allocations were corrected from 3U to 1U.

Exact 24U interior. U24 begins at the inner top rail and U1 ends at the inner bottom rail. Product faces retain source proportions; neutral chassis faces identify models without supplied orthographic assets. The four units exposed by the firewall correction are explicitly assigned to blanking panels; they are not counted as open growth capacity.

Figure 2 legend

Face treatment

  • Orthographic face — model-specific front or rear asset
  • Neutral chassis — exact orthographic asset not supplied
  • Front and rear headings state the aisle orientation

Occupancy

  • Dark outlined face — installed equipment
  • Hatched light face — passive panel, manager or blanking
  • Every one of the 24 units is assigned; no open U is encoded

U convention

  • U1 is the bottom rack unit
  • Device position is the lowest occupied U

Not encoded in Figure 2. Power-feed assignment and verified airflow direction are not yet available for every Seattle device. They remain explicit commissioning dependencies rather than inferred colors or arrows.

Appendix A

Equipment exhibit

The five device types with an orthographic face in rack SEA.MDF.R01, front and rear. Hidden by default; this is illustration, not record.

These are the same embedded orthographic faces used to draw Figure 2, presented at card scale so each chassis can be recognised on its own. They are built at page load from the figure's own asset set, so the exhibit adds no weight to the file and cannot drift from the rack.

This exhibit is not a complete bill of materials. Three of the eleven devices installed in SEA.MDF.R01 — SEA-SDW-01, SEA-SDW-02 and SEA-WLC-01 — have no orthographic face in the supplied reference.
