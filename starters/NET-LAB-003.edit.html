Section 0

Overview — what this document is

Purpose, scope, inventory posture, and the current navigation-standardization increment.

Design intent

  • Provide a portable, self-contained reference built on the Seattle 1.2 and San Jose 1.8 document grammar.
  • Show a redundant campus path from two provider edges through a firewall pair and two collapsed-core members.
  • Pair the logical view with synchronized 42U front/rear elevations using exact embedded product faces.
  • Keep unverified ports, circuits, power, airflow, and patching visibly open instead of presenting assumptions as as-built facts.

Scope & exclusions

This is a fictional training/reference design, not a construction package or operational record. Figure 1 is an L3 hierarchy view; selected Layer 2 aggregate relationships remain only where they explain redundancy, and their physical implementation stays in the engineering schedule. It does not define complete L1 patching, firewall policy, WLAN radio design, carrier handoffs, device serials, power feeds, or a bill of materials.

Revision 2.8 focus: the shared three-column Figure 1 legend, removal of redundant topology zoom controls, and a higher-contrast shared focus ring. Network geometry, rack, schedules, topology-specific colors, and semantic status colors are unchanged.

The logical view uses Cisco’s official PMS 3015 conceptual topology icons. Cisco describes these icons as globally recognized and generally accepted for network topologies, and permits use without alteration.

Rack conventions

Rack units are labeled U1 through U42 from bottom to top. The front and rear views use the same U coordinates. A device occupies the full range listed in the inventory, and ruler marks are kept outside the equipment image.

  • EIA-310 rack overview
  • Record view direction, rack ID, room, revision, and as-of date.
  • Keep planned/reserved space visually distinct from installed assets.

Diagramming conventions demonstrated

  • L3 hierarchy and supporting aggregate scope explicitly declared
  • Device IDs separate from roles
  • Trust/function boundaries labeled
  • Line semantics defined in legend
  • Subnets and VLANs use CIDR/ID notation
  • HA relationship is not a physical cable
  • Redundant links remain visually traceable
  • Front and rear U positions stay synchronized
  • Exact physical model names in inventory
  • Sources and reuse terms travel with the file

Important reuse distinction

Cisco logical symbols: use as supplied; do not redraw, recolor, reshape, or combine them into a modified icon. Resizing for placement is acceptable. Rack faces: community-maintained orthographic assets under the NetBox library’s CC0 terms. Product names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
