A · Where is your network coming from?

Reading the picture is its own AI turn, and it comes first: the AI lists every device it can see so you can correct it before anything is drawn. That corrected list is what B builds from.

New AI chat → attach the picture → paste this → send. Fix anything it misread, keep that chat open, then do B in the same chat.

Say what you have in plain English — sites, devices, what connects to what, any addresses you know. It travels inside the build prompt, so there is no first AI turn and no second chat.

The AI invents a small plausible network — one site, a handful of devices — and records in the gaps list that every value in it is invented rather than surveyed. Useful for seeing what a finished document looks like, and for having something real to edit part by part. Replace it with your own network when you are ready.

Which part is changing?

A free AI model has a limit on how much it can write in one reply. Pick the smallest part that covers your change: it only has to send that part back, so there is less to run out of room on and less it can break by accident.
