A — Have your AI read your diagram first. Copy this into a new AI chat, attach your picture, send it, then correct anything it got wrong. Keep that chat open.

B — Three questions, all optional. Leave any of them empty and the AI derives the answer from your diagram. Then press Fill in the request, Copy prompt, and paste into that same chat.

Which part is changing?

A free AI model has a limit on how much it can write in one reply. Pick the smallest part that covers your change: it only has to send that part back, so there is less to run out of room on and less it can break by accident.
